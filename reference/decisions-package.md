# decisions: Typed Decisions from Decision Models

Calls decision models, which return a probability for each allowed
answer to a typed question. Provides typed questions, deciders for
several providers, and tools to save, score and evaluate a setup.
Includes injury reports from the Severe Injury Reports of the U.S.
Occupational Safety and Health Administration
<https://www.osha.gov/severe-injury-reports>, food recall reports of the
U.S. Food and Drug Administration
<https://open.fda.gov/apis/food/enforcement/>, and simulated textile
images made with 'spc4sts' (Bui and Apley, 2021)
[doi:10.1080/00224065.2019.1707730](https://doi.org/10.1080/00224065.2019.1707730)
.
